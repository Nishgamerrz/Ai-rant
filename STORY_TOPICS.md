# Story topics

One line, one premise, one small cast. The best ones start **"In a world where …"** — they hand the AI a whole world and a hook in a single sentence, which is also the first caption on screen.

Rules of thumb:

- **One everyday problem** the cast has to decide about — not a plot with five turns.
- **A world rule that presses on ordinary life**, then one person who is out of step with it.
- **A specific ending moment**: "the ten minutes end while he is still on the ceiling", not "and he learns something".
- **Two or three people at most**, named by you, never by their usernames.

---

## In a world where … (default flavour)

1. In a world where gravity switches off for exactly ten minutes every morning, everyone plans their day around it except one boy who oversleeps.
2. In a world where no one can lie after sunset, a boy has to tell his best friend the truth before the sun goes down.
3. In a world where every promise turns into a small physical weight, one girl carries a promise she made years ago.
4. In a world where the last person to fall asleep each night gets one wish, two brothers keep each other awake on purpose.
5. In a world where everyone hears the same song at 7 a.m., one boy is the only one who hears nothing.
6. In a world where forgotten things reappear in a lost-and-found hall, a girl goes looking for a toy she threw away.
7. In a world where your shadow acts out what you really want, a shy boy's shadow starts waving at the girl he likes.
8. In a world where a door appears wherever someone is truly needed, a boy keeps being pulled away from his own life.
9. In a world where shoes remember every step, a boy borrows his brother's trainers and sees where he has been going.
10. In a world where rain falls upward, a girl runs out to catch her letter before it reaches the clouds.
11. In a world where every object holds a one-second recording of its owner's day, a boy plays back a chair nobody sat in.
12. In a world where everyone wakes up with one word written on their wrist, a boy's word is a name nobody recognises.

## Small-town everyday problems

13. In a world where the town clock runs ten minutes fast for everyone but one girl, she is late to the one thing that matters.
14. In a world where the corner shop sells only what people truly need, a boy tries to buy a present for his mother.
15. In a world where the last bus always waits for one missing passenger, a boy has to decide who to leave behind.
16. In a world where neighbours vote on every small change to the street, a boy campaigns to keep the old tree.
17. In a world where every apology has to be delivered in person before midnight, one boy runs across town.
18. In a world where a lost wallet always finds its way back, one wallet keeps returning to the person who took it.

## Friendship, family, and one choice

19. In a world where best friends share one battery between them, two boys have to decide who gets the last hour.
20. In a world where your voice sounds like whoever you are thinking about, a boy has to answer a phone call in front of his friend.
21. In a world where every mistake leaves a small mark on your hands, a boy hides his hands at dinner.
22. In a world where you can hear the house remember, a girl listens to a room that used to be her brother's.
23. In a world where the school bell rings only when someone has to confess something, the bell rings during a quiet exam.
24. In a world where people can borrow one day of someone else's life, two friends argue over who needs it more.

---

**Tip:** keep the line under about 25 words. If it needs two sentences to explain, it is two stories.
